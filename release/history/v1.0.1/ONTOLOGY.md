# Frozen ontology and counting scope

1,348 public capabilities compose through primitives, relations, invariants, laws, operations and experiences. Repeating an operation through SDK, MCP and an AI skill does not create three primitives.

The foundation register enumerates 26 primitives and 55 numbered invariants. Its heading and declared count agree with those numbered entries in `ontology/foundation-register.json`.

The current v126 registry contains 133 law entries and 132 distinct executable predicate programs. Every entry has a locally exercised rejecting witness in `ontology/registry-census.json`. These are executable predicates, not a claim of 132 mathematically independent invariants. Evaluating evidence locally does not execute a remote action offline.

The exhaustive current primitive census remains incomplete: historical name-to-implementation bindings and later semantic overlap still require qualification. This release does not claim that every historical primitive has been independently qualified offline or that generic cryptographic mechanisms were invented by Receiz. Implemented proof objects, identity, Kai coordinates and verified appends remain directly named; the uncertainty is the complete census, not those primitives.
